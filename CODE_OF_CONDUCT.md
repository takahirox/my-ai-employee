# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We welcome participation that is respectful, constructive, and inclusive. Be considerate,
accept feedback gracefully, focus criticism on ideas and code, and protect other people's
privacy. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, and publishing private
information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the security contact
described in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently exclude participants whose behavior is unsafe or disruptive.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. This policy applies in project spaces
and when someone is publicly representing the project.
